## Passes and tickets
- General pass: Anyone in tech.
- Startup pass: Founders and startup teams, from pre-seed to growth stage.
- Investor pass: VCs, angels, family offices, CVCs and operators. Includes the attending-startups database and the investor lounge.
- VIP pass: Premium, exclusive experience.
Every pass includes: 3-day access, all sessions on both stages, masterclasses (seat booking in the app), live podcasts, the Tech Expo and all common areas.
Free access: Students and accredited press (journalists covering technology and innovation; request via the press form) get free passes.
Discounts:
- Group discounts from 5+ passes.
- 15% discount for members of the Piedmont innovation hubs (Poli di Innovazione).
- Camera di commercio di Torino: 15% discount for up to 70 companies from the Turin area.
Prices: 2026 is the first edition with paid passes. Early-bird prices ended on 31 August 2026: check current prices on the official pass page. https://get.wavebyvento.com/pass (pass FAQ: https://wavebyvento.com/faq-pass)

## Stages and formats
- Fucine Stage: Main stage, 1,000+ seats.
- Binario 3 Stage: Deeper conversations and panel discussions.
- Masterclass studios: Educational sessions of 45-90 minutes, up to about 50 seats each. Seats must be reserved in the official app.
- Live podcasts: 11+ live podcast recordings with international tech voices. Bookable in the official app.
- Tech Expo: Exhibition area included in every pass.
- Investor lounge: Dedicated lounge for VCs, angels, family offices and corporate venture arms.
