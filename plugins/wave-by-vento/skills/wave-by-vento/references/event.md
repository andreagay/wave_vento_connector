# Wave by Vento 2026: practical information

## Wave by Vento 2026 (formerly Italian Tech Week)
- One of Europe's most influential tech conferences: a yearly gathering for founders, top investors and tech leaders, organised entirely by Vento.
- When: Wednesday 7 to Friday 9 October 2026. Where: OGR Torino, Corso Castelfidardo 22, Turin, a late-19th-century industrial complex next to Porta Susa train and metro station.
- Official agenda: 143 sessions (81 talks on the Fucine and Binario 3 stages, 45 masterclasses, 13 live podcasts, 4 pitch sessions) with 242 speakers. Program hours: Wed 7 Oct 10:00-17:51, Thu 8 Oct 09:00-18:02, Fri 9 Oct 09:30-16:49.

## Getting to OGR Torino
- From Porta Susa station: about 10 minutes on foot.
- From Porta Nuova station: metro to Porta Susa or Vinzaglio, both a short walk from OGR.
- Tram 9 and buses 12, 33, 58 and 68 stop at Inghilterra.
- From Turin-Caselle airport (about 16 km): Trenitalia trains to Porta Susa in about 30 minutes; Arriva/SADEM and FLIBCO buses to Porta Susa and Porta Nuova; taxis (WeTaxi app) and car rental.
- Airport shuttle by Arriva: every 15 minutes, stops at Porta Nuova and Porta Susa; round trip EUR 9.50 with the code Torino2026 while supplies last.
- By car: OGR has no parking. Nearby: Via Nino Bixio 46 (public, free), Parking Palagiustizia on Corso Vittorio Emanuele II (paid), APCOA on Corso Stati Uniti 49 (paid), Best in Parking on Corso Bolzano 47A (paid). In Turin blue lines are paid by the hour, white lines are free, yellow lines are reserved or for residents.

## Passes and tickets
- Prices on 1 October 2026, plus VAT: General EUR 500, Startup EUR 230, Investor EUR 700 (few passes left for each); VIP EUR 900 is sold out.
- Every pass: 3-day access, all agenda sessions, masterclasses and live podcasts (book them in the app), side events, tailored networking, the .WAV closing party, the Tech Expo and all common areas.
- Investor pass adds the attending-startups database and Investor Lounge access. VIP adds those plus fast-track to the main stage, priority check-in, VIP Lounge access and guaranteed seating during headliner sets.
- Categories: Startup (pre-seed to growth-stage startups), Investor (VCs, private equity, angels, CVCs, LPs, family offices and similar), General (everyone else: corporates, institutions, agencies, consultancies, non-accredited media), VIP (anyone who wants the premium experience). Organisers may check that the category matches the attendee.
- No single-day passes. Passes are personal and non-transferable, and are not refunded; name changes closed on 30 September.
- Group discounts from 5 passes until 2 October, single passes only afterwards. More than 30 passes: custom quote at wave@vento.ventures. Newsletter subscribers get a 5% discount code.
- Payment by Apple Pay, credit card or bank transfer (bank transfer only for 5+ passes, to be paid within 3 days). Invoices and VIP upgrades are in My Area.
- Free passes: accredited journalists covering technology (press form, press office wave@burlington.cc) and enrolled students (the FAQ says student passes are sold out).
Sources: [Wave by Vento - Get your pass](https://wavebyvento.com/getyourpass); [Wave by Vento - FAQs](https://wavebyvento.com/faqs); [Wave by Vento - Pass FAQ](https://wavebyvento.com/faq-pass)

## Entry and badge
- Entry: the pass QR code from My Area (linked in the purchase confirmation email; it can be saved to the phone wallet) plus an identity document, checked at the entrance.
- Badge: print it with the same QR code at the kiosks inside OGR, from a few days before the event and during the event; exact days and times are sent by email. It is valid for all three days; the help desk can print a duplicate.
- Not allowed inside: weapons and sharp objects, explosives or flammable materials, chemical sprays, alcohol, glass containers and other disruptive items such as umbrellas. Large luggage must go to the storage across the street.
- Wave does not provide visa invitation letters.
Sources: [Wave by Vento - FAQs](https://wavebyvento.com/faqs)

## Stages and formats
- Fucine: the main stage, with more than 1,000 seats.
- Binario 3: a more intimate stage for panels and deep dives on specific verticals and industry challenges.
- Room A, Room B and Room C: masterclasses, hands-on sessions in dedicated studios. Live podcasts are recorded in Room C.
- Investor Lounge: startup pitch sessions (access with Investor and VIP passes).
- Also on site: the networking area in the Duomo, the Tech Expo, the VIP Lounge and a merch shop.
Sources: [Wave by Vento - Event info](https://wavebyvento.com/event-info); [Wave by Vento - Official agenda](https://wavebyvento.com/agenda); [Wave by Vento - Get your pass](https://wavebyvento.com/getyourpass); [Wave by Vento - FAQs](https://wavebyvento.com/faqs)

## Masterclasses and live podcasts
- Masterclasses (45 to 90 minutes) and live podcasts are included in every pass but must be booked in the app because seats are limited; walk-ins are sometimes accepted when there is room.
- Masterclass slides are shared with attendees after the event; masterclasses are not recorded. Podcasters publish their episodes on their own channels.
Sources: [Wave by Vento - FAQs](https://wavebyvento.com/faqs)

- Networking area in the Duomo with numbered tables for meetings booked in the app; you can also meet in informal spaces such as the bar.
- There is no public attendee list: browse profiles in the app. The attending-startups list comes with the Investor and VIP passes.

## On-site services
- Food: Snodo (bar and buffet restaurant) inside OGR and food trucks in the west courtyard. Vegetarian and vegan options; the event-info page also mentions gluten-free.
- Your own food and drinks are allowed but can only be consumed in the outdoor front courtyard.
- Cloakroom for coats and small items at the Binario 3 entrance. Trolleys and large bags: storage at Fondazione Time2, across the street (08:30-20:00).
- Charging points in the common areas and lounges.
- Fully accessible: everything is on the ground floor, from the OGR Cult wing to the outdoor area and the Snodo bar, with step-free entrances, elevators and accessible restrooms.
